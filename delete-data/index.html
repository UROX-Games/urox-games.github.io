Crime Kingdom: delete your data

Crime Kingdom (Google Play: "Crime Kingdom: Mafia Idle RPG") by UROX Games. Last updated: September 29, 2026.

The game does not know your name, e-mail address or phone number. Everything we hold is linked to a random, anonymous identifier of your installation. You can ask us to delete it at any time, free of charge.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an e-mail to urocks.games@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data - Crime Kingdom.
  2. Include the approximate date and time when you installed the game and the model of your device. This is how we find your anonymous record.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm it by a reply to your e-mail.

What is deleted

  • gameplay and technical events (app interactions, diagnostics),
  • error and crash reports,
  • the anonymous install identifier and the anonymous account identifier stored on our server,
  • the record of your consent choices.

What may be kept

  • Records of purchases, if any, that we are legally required to keep for accounting and tax purposes, for the period required by law.
  • Aggregated statistics that cannot be linked to you or your device.

Without any request

Gameplay and technical events are deleted automatically at most 24 months after we received them.

Data on your device and data held by Google

  • Your game progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings removes it.
  • You can stop sending gameplay events at any time in the game (footer, "telemetry on/off - change").
  • Data collected by Google's advertising software is held by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google, Ads) and manage ads at myadcenter.google.com.
