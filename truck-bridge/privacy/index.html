Privacy Policy — Truck Bridge

Truck Bridge by UROX Games — Google Play package and Apple bundle ID com.Urocks.BridgeLab.

1. Who we are

Truck Bridge (the “app” or “game”) is developed and published by UROX Games (formerly “Urocks”), an independent game developer based in Slovakia, European Union. UROX Games is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for any privacy question or request: urocks.games@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress (unlocked levels, best results, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads. Our advertising partners process device and advertising identifiers and related technical data to deliver and measure ads (section 4).
  • Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details (section 5).
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not run our own servers that collect data about you.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, purchased or unlocked items, the “remove ads” status and your settings (sound, music, graphics quality, privacy choices) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

4. Advertising

Truck Bridge is free to play and is funded by ads (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads). Ads are delivered through the Unity LevelPlay mediation platform (ironSource Ltd., a Unity Technologies company) and the Unity Ads network (Unity Technologies). When the game requests or shows an ad, these partners receive the following directly from your device and process it under their own privacy policies:

  • the device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; on iOS the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt) and other device or app-scoped identifiers (for example the app set ID or IDFV);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (country or city level) location can be derived;
  • device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, the app's name and version;
  • ad interaction data: which ads were requested, shown, clicked or watched to the end, and timing information;
  • diagnostic data related to ad delivery (for example ad-loading errors).

This data is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, reward you for watched rewarded videos, prevent ad fraud, measure ad performance and, where permitted, show ads that are more relevant to you (personalised ads). Where the law requires your consent for personalised ads (for example in the EU/EEA and UK), personalised ads are shown only with that consent; otherwise contextual (non-personalised) ads are shown.

Your choices:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID”.
  • iOS: you can refuse tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Buying “Remove Ads” in the game stops interstitial and banner ads.

Partner privacy policies: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy.

5. In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases (for example removing ads). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive your name, e-mail, card number or other payment details. The game only receives a purchase confirmation (product ID and transaction token) from the store, which it uses to unlock the item and to restore purchases. Purchases are handled by the Unity In-App Purchasing library.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own, and we do not operate servers that receive data about you.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising and store partners named above.
  • The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

7. Legal bases (EU/EEA and UK)

We rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game through ads (contextual ads, fraud prevention, frequency capping and measurement), on your consent for personalised advertising where consent is required, and on the performance of a contract for in-app purchases.

8. Data retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Our advertising partners and the app stores keep the data they process for the periods described in their own privacy policies. We keep e-mails you send us only as long as needed to answer your request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to data portability. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, requests about advertising data are best sent to the partners above together with your advertising ID. You can also write to us at urocks.games@gmail.com and we will help. California residents: we do not sell personal information; you can opt out of personalised ads using the device settings above. EU residents have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Slovakia: Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).

10. Children

Truck Bridge is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, please contact us and we will take steps to have it deleted.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date below shows when it was last updated; for material changes we will also inform players in the store listing or in the game.
